**What is PARA?**
PARA is a method for organizing digital files, created from the starting letters of its four components:
- **P - Projects:** Short-term efforts that have a clear goal and a specific deadline.
- **A - Areas:** Long-term responsibilities that do not have a clear goal or deadline. Most of the time, an Area will include many Projects.
- **R - Resource:** Topics or interests that are useful but do not currently fall under a Project or Area.
- **A - Archive:** Inactive items, including completed projects, areas you are no longer interested in, or resources that are no longer useful.

**Key Principles:**
- **Cross-Platform:** This structure can be applied across all platforms, from Google Drive to your local laptop, creating an easy-to-maintain system.
- **Flexible:** You do not need to follow it strictly; it can be scaled up or down based on your actual needs.
- **Self-Reflection:** Using this system helps you learn more about how you work. Checking the archive provides a clear look back at your past efforts.

**How to Start:**
- **The "Clean Slate" Approach:** The easiest way to start is to move all current, old files into a new folder with today's date, and place that into the "Archive" folder. Only work with new files in the PARA system going forward.
- **Create on Demand:** Do not pre-create folders for projects or resources you think you might need in the future. Only create folders when you actually touch the material.
